Mountain safety guide

Preparation lowers avoidable risk; it cannot remove objective hazards. Use the notes below as prompts for a route-specific plan with qualified people.

Before leaving

  • Check the latest local forecast, avalanche bulletin, route conditions and permit rules.
  • Share your route and return time; carry a way to communicate and navigate if electronics fail.
  • Confirm rescue and evacuation insurance, first-aid supplies and a team turnaround time.
  • Do not ascend with altitude symptoms. Worsening symptoms at rest, confusion or breathlessness require urgent descent and medical care.

This page is general information, not medical or professional climbing advice. For altitude illness, consult a qualified clinician. Call local emergency services in an emergency.
