About Alpine Ascents

Alpine Ascents is a mountaineering information and learning website: a place to explore mountain routes, understand climbing styles and prepare better questions before a real trip.

Explore route profiles, mountaineering field notes and an optional nearby-club search using public map data. This site is not a guiding company, emergency service or booking operator.

Start with the mountaineering field guide, compare expedition routes, or review the mountain safety guide.
